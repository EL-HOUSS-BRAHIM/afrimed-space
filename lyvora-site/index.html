BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A little more clarity.

A few things to know about the Android preview.

Is Lyvora available for my phone?

The preview is an APK for Android phones using ARM64. An iPhone or web app is not available here. Visit the download page for installation instructions.

Does it connect to my bank?

Lyvora uses manual entry. You record your accounts, income, spending, bills and goals yourself; it does not import bank transactions.

What does “preview” mean?

This is an early version that is still changing. The preview uses a separate account from the regular Lyvora app. Review your entries and keep your own financial records.

Who is building Lyvora?

Lyvora is built by Brahim El Houss ↗ and is part of Afrimed Space ↗, a shared home for three people's individual and collaborative projects.

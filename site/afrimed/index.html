01 / The vision

A vision for more approachable healthcare.

The following describes the project's direction. Features are in development and may evolve before launch.

01

Health technology

Exploring AI-assisted medical image analysis as part of a broader healthcare experience, with responsible development at its core.

02

Connections to care

Developing ways to discover healthcare providers and bring people closer to useful care information.

03

Community support

Working toward medical-aid transparency and a place to follow health information and personal records.

02 / The story

03 / A few answers

Before the next chapter.

Is Afrimed available now?

The public experience is coming soon. This page introduces the project and its direction; it does not provide clinical services.

Can I upload scans or get a diagnosis here?

No. This website does not accept medical records, analyse scans, or provide a diagnosis. For medical advice, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.

When will the project launch?

There is no announced public launch date. More details will be shared here as the project develops.
